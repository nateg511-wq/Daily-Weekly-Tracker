Iran hitting Duqm days before its own FM cites Oman as an active mediator (Jul 13) is either strategic incoherence or a signal that Tehran no longer sees the mediation channel as sacred — either reading should worry a dip-buy thesis more than the market currently prices.
  • It keeps breaking, and now it's bigger. Four strike rounds in a week, ~300 cumulative targets, the first sea-launched attack drones, and the first strike on a Gulf mediator state — each escalation is larger and more novel than the last, directly contradicting "time heals."   • The casualty and inflow data could reverse fast. Iran's own casualty claim has nearly doubled in under a week (14/78 → 17/115); if that trend continues or a US servicemember is confirmed killed, the "zero US casualties" anchor holding the long-term score at 83 disappears overnight, and the ETF-inflow counter-signal could reverse just as quickly.
  • Thin sample, still. The bullish long-term read rests on ~2 dip-and-recover events, both in a bull regime — geopolitical shocks don't always V-recover, and this is now the most novel escalation sequence of the year.
