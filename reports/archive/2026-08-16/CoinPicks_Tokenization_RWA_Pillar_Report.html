The Other Side

The strongest honest case this is wrong — updated August 16, 2026, light daily touch
  • Two data points isn't a trend, and both are one-degree removed from crypto-native. A Central Bank of Ireland approval and a Ripple equity stake in two UK fintechs are real, but they're incremental institutional-infrastructure moves, not evidence of accelerating adoption — the +1/+1 nudge reflects that, but even a small move risks reading too much into routine business development.
  • The RWA all-time high is still a small, largely institutional/permissioned number. $38.24B in total distributed value is real growth, but it's still under 1% of the BCG×Ripple $9.4T-by-2030 TAM anchor, and this pillar has twice now had to correct press coverage that inflated or mislabeled RWA figures — a reminder that headline numbers in this space need active skepticism, not just repetition.
  • Regulatory limbo is still real. The GENIUS Act's rulemaking deadline was missed and zero of six agencies have finalized rules, with comment windows only now closing (FDIC Aug 4, CIP Aug 21) — nearly seven months of mid-market issuers operating under an incomplete rulebook, which can chill activity rather than de-risk it.
  • The Represented-vs-Distributed value gap is still doing heavy lifting for the bull case. The much larger ~$373.55B "Represented Value" figure is mostly locked or permissioned tokenization, not liquid or retail-accessible — a distinct metric this report is careful not to conflate with the $38.24B distributed-value ATH.
  • It may not be "crypto." Most volume is permissioned/institutional and may never accrue value to crypto majors. Durable market-structure law (CLARITY) still isn't passed, and its own prediction-market odds hit a fresh record low this week.
