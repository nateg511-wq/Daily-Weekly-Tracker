A durable ceasefire has not been demonstrated; only a pattern of attacks-then-lulls-then-more-attacks has.
  • Thin sample, still. The bullish long-term read rests on a handful of dip-and-recover events, all in a bull regime — geopolitical shocks don't always V-recover, and a diplomatic framework that isn't signed yet is not obviously more durable than the Jul 25-27 lull that broke within days.
